Data origin

The data is automatically extracted from the BIG-IP and F5OS "Fixes and Known Issues" pages published by F5, Inc. on techdocs.f5.com, and from F5's public Bug Tracker (cdn.f5.com). Each bug links to the page of the official note mentioning it and to its Bug Tracker page. Support dates of the branches come from F5 article K5903, the platforms of each release from article K9412, the latest point releases from article K9502, the hardware lifecycle from article K4309 the current releases from article K2200, the F5OS support dates from article K21501912 and the F5OS releases of each platform from article K86001294, published on my.f5.com.
